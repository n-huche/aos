# Page Manager

## Subscription options

- **Free:** 1 page, up to 50 images, and 500,000 characters, for 48 hours at no cost.
- **Essential:** 1 page, up to 150 images, and 1,500,000 characters per page, for R$3/month.
- **Trio:** up to 3 pages, up to 150 images, and 1,500,000 characters per page, for R$5/month.
- **Studio:** up to 10 pages, up to 300 images, and 3,000,000 characters per page, for R$10/month.
- **Flexible:** no fixed limit on pages or content. The user is charged based on resource usage over the previous month: R$1 per page/month, R$0.20 per 100 images/month, and R$0.02 per million characters/month.

## Rules

Any user can create their first page for free and keep it active for 48 hours.

After the 48-hour period, the user can start a subscription to keep the page active continuously for as long as the subscription remains valid.

If the user does not start a subscription, the page will remain available for recovery for 7 days. After this period, its content will be permanently deleted.

Users can freely create, edit, and delete pages, subject to the page, image, and character limits defined by their subscription.

Users can track the number of images and characters used on each page, as well as the total number of images and characters used across all their pages.

On the Flexible plan, users can also track how their usage of pages, images, and characters affects their billing.

# Page Editor

At the top of the page, the editor displays the content limits defined by the user's subscription, showing the number of images and characters used and the remaining allowance.

Users can freely add, modify, and remove text and images. The editor provides content suggestions for each section, but these suggestions do not restrict what users can enter.

Users can select one font for headings and another for body text. Text fields support headings, subheadings, and body text, including **bold** and _italic_ formatting. Font sizes are determined by the page layout and cannot be adjusted individually.

Users can select a three-color palette — primary, secondary, and tertiary — which is applied consistently across the page. The primary color is used for headings and clickable elements; the secondary color is used for subheadings and non-clickable elements; and the tertiary color predominates in the background, with automatically generated variations to ensure contrast.

The page is divided into four sections: Home, Diary, Memories, and Playlist. Home is mandatory and contains no specific items of its own. Diary, Memories, and Playlist are optional and contain their own distinct items. Users can choose which sections to include.

Items can be placed in folders or subfolders. Users can freely arrange the order of folders and items, except for Diary entries, which are automatically ordered by their assigned date.

## Home

- Couple name, a heart, and a text field
- Semi-transparent background (optional)
- Relationship day counter (optional)

## Diary

- The Diary consists of entries.
- Each entry can contain an image and a text field.
- The default entry title is the assigned date.
- Users can change the entry title.
- Entries are organized by their assigned date.

## Memories

- Memories consist of moments.
- Each moment can contain an image and a text field.
- The user must provide a title for each moment.

## Playlist

- The Playlist consists of songs.
- Users must provide a Spotify link to add a song.
- Each song can contain a text field.
- The song title is its name.
